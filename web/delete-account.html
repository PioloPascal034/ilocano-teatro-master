Account deletion

Request removal of your account.

There is currently no in-app account-deletion request form. Submit a request by email so ownership can be verified safely.

Developer: MR Bisac Studios

How to request deletion

Email marcronielbisac@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account.

Include “Ilocano Teatro Master account deletion request” in the subject and provide your account email address. Do not send your password, access token, or other secret credentials.

What the request covers

After account ownership is verified, the request will be reviewed for deletion of the account and associated application data held for that account, including profile information, teacher/student connections, learning progress, quiz activity, Theme Builder drafts and submissions, review feedback, notifications, Story Vault reading progress, and private avatar media where the data model and access controls support removal.

Possible exceptions

Some information may need to be retained where required for security, legal obligations, dispute resolution, or reliable service records. Any applicable exception will be explained in the response. This page does not promise a fixed processing time because a verified timeline has not been established.

Before submitting

  • Confirm that you are emailing from the account address or can provide another safe way to verify ownership.
  • Download or preserve any work you need before requesting deletion.
  • Understand that deletion may remove drafts, submissions, progress, quiz history, feedback, notifications, and private media associated with the account.
